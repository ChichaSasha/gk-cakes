## Context and options

Durable local collection/refund operations with provider idempotency and reconciliation versus synchronous best-effort calls. Gross paid amount and payment fees are separate.

## Recommendation

One order lock arbitrates production/cancellation. Pending and unknown refunds reserve captured balance; provider uncertainty never frees that balance. Customer cancellation checks positive eligible states. Snapshot arithmetic and allocation, not current catalog prices.

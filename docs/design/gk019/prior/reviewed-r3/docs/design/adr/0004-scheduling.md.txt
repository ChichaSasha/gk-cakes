## Context and options

## Decision proposed for technical acceptance

Seven cakes per handover date is a soft standard workload limit. Tentative demand is advisory; already-admitted genuine full payments may exceed it. Physical ready stock remains exclusive. Chef extras outside standard count; reducing limit preserves commitments. No per-slot count cap.192h ordinary minimum and original120h refund deadline are distinct.

## Inventory and grocery rules

1. Canonical quantities use exact decimal storage/arithmetic with a documented
   allowed scale selected from chef unit examples; wire quantities are decimal
   strings, not binary floats. Count may require integers; conversion values are
   positive bounded exact ratios. g↔kg is dimensional; g↔ml requires recorded
   ingredient-specific evidence. Never infer density or round a shortage away.
2. Receipt is positive, use/waste negative, adjustment explicitly signed with
   reason, reversal opposes a referenced movement and cannot reverse it twice or
   beyond its unreversed quantity. Lot balance and current version are checked
   under locks. Recommended initial policy rejects below-zero final balance with
   an actionable discrepancy; alternative explicit negative adjustments remain
   a Creator choice. No clipping balances to zero or silent stock correction.
3. All order-linked use callers lock order first, then inventory ingredient/lot
   rows in stable ID order and append movement/audit/command atomically. Pure
   receipt/adjustment only locks those inventory rows and never acquires an order
   afterward. K04 r3 places inventory rows after resources/reservations and before
   appended command/history/audit/notification records. Stale
   adjustment returns 409 and nothing is written. No provider call under lock.
4. Planned demand is a forecast of committed, not cancelled, made-to-order item
   recipe snapshots. Include not-yet-recorded consumption even if a production
   transition already happened: in-production/ready/completed with missing usage
   appears as “usage not recorded”, not erased demand. Unpaid holds/draft quotes
   have separate optional projections and never inflate the committed total.
5. Outstanding demand = snapshot planned quantity minus net linked actual use,
   floored at zero for future demand only; excess actual use is separately shown
   as variance. Physical ledger balances are never floored. A partial 300g use
   against 800g plan leaves 500g demand and already reduces stock by 300g: do not
   allocate the full 800g again. Waste is not recipe use and does not satisfy demand.
6. Cancellation removes remaining commitment; actual used/wasted goods stay used.
   Refund itself changes neither ledger nor demand. Ready-made sale reserves
   finished stock only; prior batch use is not another future recipe demand.
   Substitution/scaling changes needing allergen agreement use an explicitly
   accepted order revision; never mutate an old purchased recipe version.
7. Recommended simplest consumption is chef-confirmed actual use with prefilled
   snapshot quantities. Alternative automatic movement at production start uses
   the unique production transition and may be corrected by linked adjustments;
   Creator must choose before either ships. Timed/manual start is not evidence
   that every ingredient has physically been used. X and ready-stock production
   boundary belong to Orders/Creator, not Inventory.
8. Compute one consistent DB snapshot for a max31-day request. Starting usable
   stock accounts for commitments before `from` that remain unconsumed, otherwise
   selecting a later window would double-spend stock. Allocate chronological
   demand by production day then stable order-item ID, earliest valid expiry lot
   first. Treat expiry as usable-through or unusable-on according to approved
   policy; unknown expiry yields uncertainty until resolved. Allocation is a
   read-only planning result, not a fulfillment promise or lot reservation.
9. Per date show cumulative on-hand allocation, outstanding demand and shortage.
   Future supplier purchases are not on-hand; no purchasing workflow is implied.
   Budget uses shortage × as-of unit-cost ratio, rounded up to pence per ingredient
   estimate so fractional cost is not understated; sum displayed rounded values.
   Example shortage 500g at 240p/1000g estimates 120p. Pack size/minimum-order,
   delivery fees, tax treatment and missing prices are stated assumptions, not
   invented exact purchase cost; incomplete total is labelled a known subtotal.

## Notification dispatch, identity and recovery

Orders/Payments produce unique milestones: order_confirmed after verified capture,
order_cancelled after committed cancellation, refund_requested for an accepted
refund operation/goal, refund_confirmed only after authoritative success, and an
approved correction/action-required notice if later evidence changes that truth.
Refund goal versus operation wording must distinguish partial and full completion.
Other status/pickup notices reuse their transition identities. A repeated webhook
with a new event ID but same business milestone cannot create a second notice.
Customer message templates show saved order amounts/timezone; they do not promise
inbox arrival, bank posting date or that cancellation alone returned money.

The required state, intent, job and audit commit in the producer's single DB
transaction under K08 r3; there is no postcommit enqueue gap.
Provider invocation occurs afterward. Internal worker has transport capability,
not permission to create arbitrary customer email or change contact ownership.

Claim increments job generation and sets owner/lease atomically. Every finalization,
retry schedule and intent state update compares current owner + generation + live
lease. Domain reconciliation separately increments intent reconciliation generation;
an older fetch result cannot overwrite newer authoritative evidence. A worker
checks authority immediately before dispatch, but a lease cannot cancel a request
already in flight. Overlapping workers use the same provider idempotency key and
immutable payload. Fencing prevents stale local writes, not duplicate external
delivery by itself; provider capability/retention verification is a release gate.

Before sending, store the stable send identity and first-dispatch instant. Known
acceptance records provider ID; accepted does not imply delivered. Timeout/crash
becomes unknown after lease recovery. Retry can call provider create only while
its verified idempotency guarantee still covers the first call and same payload;
otherwise use status lookup/provider events or manager investigation. A provider
without sufficient dedup/status capability requires an approved restricted unknown
recovery flow, never blind retry presented as exactly once. Retry retention must
be measured from first dispatch and leave allowance for provider/time uncertainty.

Verified webhook receipt validates signature, replay tolerance under selected
provider contract, account/environment and mapped object. Persist unique inbox
receipt before 2xx; DB failure returns retriable failure. Unknown object before
send response remains pending correlation by stable operation metadata/provider
lookup, not silently dropped. Invalid signature does not enqueue or mutate status.
Events append evidence with provider event time and received time. Effective view
retains delivered_to_server and subsequent bounce/adverse facts; a later-received
stale accepted event cannot erase bounce. Ordering uncertainties show “Checking”.

Manager preview binds order/customer association, contact ID + version, exact
recipient address snapshot, subject/body/template revision and manager identity
into server-held preview evidence. Hash alone is not authorization. Send reloads
all bound records and rechecks active manager role, allowed transactional purpose,
order/contact version and content. Recipient input from browser cannot redirect
the send. No auto-linking or email-string matching. A changed/deleted contact,
relinked order, edited content or revoked role invalidates preview and creates no
intent. Signed-out staff retain no server-authorized send action.

Accepted intent freezes its recipient; worker never follows a later profile email
silently. Contact revocation/privacy deletion and any post-enqueue recipient safety
change suspend unsent dispatch for manager review under contact-generation check;
they cannot recall mail already accepted by provider. Correcting recipient uses
the Identity/Orders ownership workflow, then a new preview and linked deliberate
send. Unknown prior send is reconciled first; if irreducible, explicit manager
duplicate-risk acknowledgement and reason creates a successor intent. UI shows
both attempts. Reconciliation alone cannot resend or modify business/payment state.
No marketing/bulk send capability is inferred by A11.

## Finance, business activity and site metrics

Reports are read-only projections of authoritative facts, not a new financial
ledger. Query one consistent snapshot; include `as_of`, requested London dates,
computed UTC bounds, source freshness and completeness reasons. Ranges are local
calendar dates inclusive at UI; SQL is `[start-of-from, start-of-day-after-to)` in
Europe/London, allowing 23/25-hour days. Max31 local dates, not 31×24 hours.

- Cash activity counts verified captures by capture instant and verified successful
  refunds by refund-effective instant within the window. Cross-period refunds may
  produce negative net receipts; do not clamp. Captured amounts already incorporate
  checkout discount. `net_customer_receipts = gross_captured - successful_refunds`.
  Snapshot discounts associated with captures in the window display separately;
  subtracting them again would be wrong. Quote/draft discounts are excluded.
- Known fees and fee adjustments use their own observed effective date and currency;
  `net_after_known_fees = net_customer_receipts - known_net_fees`. Unknown fees are
  null/incomplete, not zero. This is neither profit, tax filing, bank balance nor
  provider payout. Inventory estimates cannot become cost-of-goods accounting.
- Provider observations from financial-pilot r3 are real money evidence even when
  not admitted into the controlled refund-allocation ledger. Union confirmed local
  and verified external facts by provider/account/environment/refund identity;
  matched observation contributes once. Unmatched success contributes observed
  returned money and a reconciliation warning; contradictory status, amount or
  identity makes affected aggregates incomplete rather than guessed. Preserve all
  evidence, not two competing numeric truths. Disputes/chargebacks and duplicate
  captures are separately identified observed flows/discrepancies until accepted
  accounting treatment exists; never silently fold them into refunds or omit them.
- Show local returned, local reserved/unknown, separately observed unmatched
  returns, quarantined capture count, refund-goal shortfalls and worst-case exposure
  on the recovery drilldown. `available=0` for quarantined capture regardless of
  subtraction. Example capture 10000p, unknown local 10000p, distinct external
  succeeded refund 5000p: observed receipts 5000p with incomplete reconciliation,
  local reservation 10000p, possible exposure 15000p, available 0. If both distinct
  refunds are proven successful, recorded observed returned 15000p and receipts
  -5000p remain visible with over-refund discrepancy; capped allocations must not
  erase provider truth. Matching the same refund later must not double count it.
- Business activity defines orders created, payment-confirmed, cancelled, completed
  by their transition timestamp; selected-window refund cash activity is not an
  order-cohort report. Source filters distinguish website/provider payments and
  approved manual/offline records. No offline record is labelled Stripe collected;
  unknown offline return remains separate from verified provider money.
- Website pageviews/visits use the selected analytics provider's exact documented
  definition and bot/privacy filters. They are not unique human daily users or
  signed-in account counts. A05's desired daily-users meaning is an open Creator
  decision; visits cannot silently satisfy it. Reuse provider dashboard initially
  if approved; website admin API returns unavailable with reason until a supported
  integration/definition is accepted. No new identifying cookies, IP storage,
  fingerprinting or customer/order fields in analytics without privacy decision.

Proposed money payload for the known fixture (10000p capture, 2000p succeeded refund,
100p known fees, already-applied 1000p discount):

```json
{"currency":"GBP","gross_captured_minor":10000,"successful_refunds_minor":2000,"net_customer_receipts_minor":8000,"discounts_minor":1000,"known_net_fees_minor":100,"net_after_known_fees_minor":7900,"complete":true,"incomplete_reasons":[],"pending_refunds_minor":0,"quarantined_capture_count":0,"as_of":"2026-09-21T12:00:00Z"}
```

`complete` concerns the selected defined data sources/window, not accounting or
legal completeness. Fee availability/reconciliation source must support that claim.

## Proposed API detail and errors

All endpoints use `/api/v1`, K00 error envelope, UUID IDs and server-generated
request IDs. Manager-only unless specified; 401/403, not-owned/unknown 404, and
private `Cache-Control: no-store`. Reads use cursor default20/max50, date reads
max31 days. Mutation keys scope actor+command+resource; same fingerprint returns
saved result even if current version changed, changed payload yields 409 KEY_REUSED.
New key stale mutation yields 409 STALE_VERSION. Other omitted fields are optional
only when stated below; null is rejected unless explicitly allowed.

| Contract / producer→consumer | Request, required fields and response | Validation/failure/recovery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /admin/inventory` / Inventory→A-INVENTORY | Optional ingredient_id/cursor; returns rows ingredient/lot identity, unit, signed decimal balance, expiry date or null, version, as_of; finished-stock section uses authoritative stock projection | Unknown expiry marked; no summed g+ml. 503 retry read; permission errors cannot expose contacts. |

## Platform, recovery and performance

Use [architecture](../architecture.md) budgets as proposals: 500 products, 10000
historical orders, 20 readers/5 checkout attempts; API read p95≤300ms, local
mutation p95≤500ms and dashboard p95≤1s excluding providers. Add an explicit
synthetic operations fixture of 200 ingredients, 2000 lots, 100000 movements,
100 outstanding orders, 500 due jobs and 31-day demand. These are test workloads,
not observed capacity. Measure query plans/rows scanned, p95 lock wait and whole
page time on selected compute before approving budgets. Index observed query
shapes listed above; no warehouse/materialized totals until measurement warrants it.

K00/architecture supplies 10s API, 2s DB lock/statement, 5s provider request, 60s
lease with heartbeat/fencing and at most three DB conflict retries inside deadline.
Jobs use bounded batches (propose 20 per claim) and 1m/5m/15m/1h retry delays then
manager attention, always subject to unknown-result rules. Five-minute scheduling
cadence and urgent oldest-work alert at15m are proposed detection targets, not
promised email latency. Record actual scheduling delay, send acceptance delay,
due age and exhausted/unknown count. No retry storm or unbounded backlog loop.

API and maintenance use the same immutable backend artifact/schema compatibility.
UI has a separate artifact/target/credentials. Neither main CI nor a branch push
deploys production. In nonproduction validate the proposed `ui-v`/`api-v` release
published routing, tag→exact trusted-main SHA, successful required main CI for
that SHA, immutable digest and artifact provenance. Separate authorized release
publication is mandatory. Serialize per environment/component and gate database
migrations globally; replay has one release identity and no repeated migration.
Queued older release cannot downgrade newer deployment implicitly. Do not cancel
an in-flight migration when a new event arrives.

Migration role differs from app/backup roles. Expand schema before compatible API,
then UI, observe old clients and separately approve cleanup. Test empty→current
and prior-supported→current migration plus current/previous UI/API compatibility.
Roll back code to a proven compatible digest; do not destroy new data via blind
down migration. New API capabilities cannot require simultaneous UI release.
PR jobs have no production secrets, immutable reviewed Actions pins and minimum
permissions; selected workload identity or scoped environment secret verifies
target/environment. Production push-deploy hooks must be off before activation.

Recovery runbook is a production gate, not an implementation performed here:

1. Creator approves RPO/RTO, backup retention and incident/financial owner; verify
   selected DB and object backup facilities, encrypted off-site access and costs.
2. Restore to isolated DB/storage with no production send/payment credentials or
   outbound dispatch; record backup timestamp/schema and actual duration/loss.
   Test purchased snapshots, images/private ACL, movements/balances, inbox identities,
   pending jobs, audit and command uniqueness, plus deletion/revocation replay.
3. A restore may predate an external success. Increment deployment/dispatch epoch,
   revoke old worker authority and keep payment/email creation frozen. Reconcile
   the entire lost-time window's provider captures/refunds/sends against restored
   IDs; recover external observations and quarantine ambiguity. Email dedup keys
   may be expired; a restored queued intent is not permission to send it anew.
4. Evaluate outstanding resources/expired holds and restored identity roles/tokens
   using their owner contracts; never accept traffic from stale restored promises.
   Manager reconciles unknowns, approves unfreeze within incident authorization,
   then controlled smoke checks establish actual running UI/API/schema/objects.
   Unresolved financial or email identities remain individually quarantined.

Operational alerts include missed scheduler, oldest unresolved urgent operation,
repeated failures, provider inbox backlog, backup failure and budget threshold;
prefer current providers. Controlled alert test must reach the named incident owner.
Logs carry correlation IDs; recovery queues link authorized domain operations,
never instruct a nontechnical chef to edit database/provider balances blindly.

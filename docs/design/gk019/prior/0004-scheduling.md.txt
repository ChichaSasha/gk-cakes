## Context and options

Separate production-day capacity, finite ready-stock and pickup appointment resources versus one calendar counter. Owner rejects automatic complexity weights. The [GK-001 handover](../../creator/GK-001/handover.md) now specifies seven cakes per handover date, two cakes using two places; chef extras are outside the standard count and both simultaneous last-place orders may proceed. No separate pickup order cap. Reducing the limit to five with seven cakes accepted preserves all seven; only the chef personally may cancel an accepted order. Strict admission mechanics still need GK-019 integration. Minimum lead time is 192 elapsed hours; uncertain dates require personal chef/admin agreement. Refund deadline is saved from original handover minus 120 elapsed hours and never moves with the appointment.

## Recommendation

Reserve resources atomically, keep expired/unknown payment commitments until terminal-unpaid evidence or explicit recoverable exception. Pickup move acquires target before releasing old in one transaction; never move production implicitly. Ready stock does not consume new production capacity.

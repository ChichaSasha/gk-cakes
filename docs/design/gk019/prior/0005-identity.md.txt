## Context and options

Managed identity with local active roles versus custom passwords; user-sent Instagram summary versus unsupported website-as-user messaging.

## Recommendation

Verify token issuer/audience/signature/expiry, then role and resource ownership. Never link historical orders by typed email or Instagram handle alone. Copy-summary/open-profile is fallback, not proof of sending; retain a recoverable draft.

statutory rights, seller/contact particulars and privacy obligations. No unresolved
launch-blocking finding. VAT status/treatment is rechecked as applicable; no
exemption or tax rate is guessed. Revised policies are versioned and existing
orders retain their accepted commitments.

AC2. Actual technology inventory records purpose, provider, retention, sharing,
transfers, necessary/optional classification and applicable consent/objection
controls. Evidence verifies data minimisation, scoped staff access, deletion,
withdrawal and backup expiry/reapplication. Policies match: orders two years;
unconverted enquiries six months from last substantive customer conversation;
personal photos 30 days or specifically permitted use three months from handover,
earlier withdrawal/removal and necessary private dispute evidence; inactive account
24 months then warning plus 30 days; device favourites one year; non-sensitive drafts
30 days; separate financial retention; service email only; aggregate public-page
metrics with no private account/admin/checkout routes, ads tracking or recordings.
Exact provider behaviour and controls are checked, not inferred from marketing.

AC3. Owner verifies actual ingredients and allergen declarations, including bought-in
components and decorations. Confirmed matrix: all flavours EGGS/MILK/SOYA/WHEAT;
Snickers adds PEANUTS, Chocolate Oreo adds SULPHITES; shared-kitchen notice separate.
All sizes use Swiss meringue buttercream; sugar pearls/fruit/berries and any other
chef-agreed decoration are included in the actual-order check. Approved composition
changes require customer agreement; otherwise original cake if feasible or full
refund. Purchase content and final box label match the exact order and applicable
labelling requirements. Record sample label/version and before-purchase evidence.

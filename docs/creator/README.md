report exactly what is missing and continue unaffected work.

## Two human roles

Use the [developer/business-owner split](../roles/README.md). For an owner handoff,
preserve the original Ukrainian answer and its source/version, then record the
checked English interpretation for technical consumers. Business approval belongs
to the owner or an explicitly recorded delegate; technical configuration evidence
belongs to the developer. Do not attribute one person's decisions to the other.
